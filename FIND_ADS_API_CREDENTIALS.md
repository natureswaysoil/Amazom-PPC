# How to Find Your Amazon Ads API Credentials

## Direct URLs to Try (in order):

### Option 1: Amazon Advertising API Console (Most Likely)
**https://advertising.amazon.com/cm/app/settings**

Or try:
- https://advertising.amazon.com/API
- https://advertising.amazon.com/cm/app/api

### Option 2: Amazon Seller Central (If you're a seller)
**https://sellercentral.amazon.com/apps/manage**

Then look for:
- "Manage Your Apps"
- "Developer Central"
- Any app related to "Advertising API" or "PPC"

### Option 3: Amazon Developer Console
**https://developer.amazon.com/settings/console/securityprofile/overview.html**

Look for a Security Profile specifically named with "Ads" or "Advertising" in the name.

### Option 4: Check Your Email

Search your email (natureswaysoil@gmail.com) for:
- Subject: "Amazon Ads API" 
- Date: Around August 2, 2025
- From: ads-api-onboarding@amazon.com

The approval email should contain:
- Links to access your credentials
- Instructions for getting your Client ID/Secret
- Or the credentials themselves

### Option 5: Contact Amazon Ads API Support

If you can't find the credentials, email:
**ads-api-onboarding@amazon.com**

Say:
"I received API access approval on August 2, 2025 but cannot locate my Client ID, Client Secret, and Refresh Token. Please provide instructions for accessing these credentials."

## What Specifically to Look For

When you find the right page, you should see:
- **LWA Client Identifier** (this is the Client ID)
- **LWA Client Secret** (this is the Client Secret)  
- **Refresh Token** or a button to "Generate Refresh Token"

These are specifically for the **Amazon Advertising API**, not general Login with Amazon.
